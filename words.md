Eg. Recently, a proposal has *surfaced* that ... .

最近出现一项提议:...

**maintain v.坚持认为，主张**

eg. I maintain that ...

我坚持：...

**initiative n.提议，举措**

eg. While the initiative sounds good, it actually is a double-edge-sword.

尽管提议听起来不错，这实际上是个双刃剑。

eg. hand-on experience 

亲身经历

**mandatory adj.强制性的**

eg. The mandatory arrangement makes everyone upset.

强制性的安排让所有人都很失落。

eg. implement plan 

执行计划
