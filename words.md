**surface v.显露、重新出现**

Eg. Recently, a proposal has *surfaced* that ... .

最近出现一项提议:...

**maintain v.坚持认为，主张**

eg. I maintain that ...

我坚持：...

**initiative n.提议，举措**

eg. While the initiative sounds good, it actually is a double-edge-sword.

尽管提议听起来不错，这实际上是个双刃剑。

**hand-on adj.亲身实践的**

eg. hand-on experience 

亲身经历

**mandatory adj.强制性的**

eg. The mandatory arrangement makes everyone upset.

强制性的安排让所有人都很失落。

**implement v.实施，执行**

eg. implement plan 

执行计划

**impose v.强制实行，迫使**

组合：impose ... on sb./sth.

将...施加在...

eg. This system imposes additional financial burdens on many people.

这个制度给很多人增加了额外的经济负担。

**one-size-fits-all adj.一刀切的、完全之策、通用的**

eg. Government should implement it flexibly rather than imposing a one-size-fits-all rule.

政府应该灵活实行，而非强制实行一刀切的规定。
